About
Smart Electric Skateboard is a professional telemetry and ride analytics system developed as part of an engineering research project.

Developed by Poojitha Dilshan

The system combines ESP32 communication, VESC telemetry, ride tracking, battery monitoring, motor monitoring and performance analytics.
